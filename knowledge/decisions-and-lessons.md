## Working grimoire

The research discussion treats craftsmanship, construction, and movement as a coupled design problem. The current governing user brief is The Anatomy & Physics of the Book (Andre Olivier, 24 October 2026). Build outward from the sewn, rounded/backed text block; do not force it into the ranked-cover envelope or combine a hollow-back case with a laced-board tight back implicitly.

V1’s mixed folds and unclear attachment path were retired. Current V2 selects a sewn-on-tapes hollow-back case, with separate block spine, hollow, card/case spine, board joints, mull flanges, and endpaper hinges. Derive 38.4 mm writing paper + 0.36 mm free endpapers + proposed 3.3 mm concentrated fold swell. A 240-leaf book has 480 writing pages. The proposed 24 gatherings contain five nested bifolia/ten leaves each; this is not asserted as universal imposition practice.

Closed topology does not establish clearance. Intermediate opening tests found crowded paper; further tests found an incorrectly shrinking case curve. Preserve arc length, physical leaf identity, a closed normal-offset turning leaf, endpoint refinements, and distinct source geometry versus distant-render proxies. Clasps must release before dropping; closing settles an interrupted leaf first. Static anatomy knowledge and authored kinematics do not simulate moisture, anisotropy, adhesives, fatigue, gravity, or a real sewn dummy.

Earlier material restoration, endpaper overlap, fore-edge strap intrusion, moiré, and page-turn performance findings remain part of the record. Device-specific performance observations are scoped measurements. V1 audits do not certify V2; run the applicable V2 topology, motion, interfaces, browser, and sandbox checks.
