# Personas and expert lenses

These are design/review perspectives, not claims that independent human specialists approved the models. Codex produced the public assistant responses. Andre Olivier is the user/brief author. Relevant original language remains in the conversation records and governing binding knowledge.

| Object | Perspectives and source | What each lens asks |
|---|---|---|
| Ranked cover system | Reference analyst, design-system author, technical artist. These three indexing labels are authored for this migration; no separate experts are claimed. | What is visible versus interpreted? Are rank, element, motif, style, and industry independent? Do SVG geometry, 3D geometry, motion, labels, evidence status, and exports agree? |
| Working grimoire | Conservator, binder, paper scientist, production engineer, book designer, structural engineer, technical artist; explicitly recorded in shared binding knowledge/generated anatomy and the related conversation. | Trace attachment/load paths and reversible repair layers; separate binding families; respect grain/caliper/swell; establish tolerances and a sewn dummy; maintain gutter legibility; account for gravity/bending/cyclic strain; inspect actual thickness, pivots, backs, and moving attachments. |
| Bound open book | Close-up geometry inspector and integration designer. Migration indexing labels derived from the room upgrade discussion. | Does accumulated paper have volume, do leaves have edges/backs, are boards/endpapers/headbands separate, and can the object be inspected in its surrounding room? |

Creative/fantasy expression remains distinct from engineering confidence. A convincing story, attractive reference, closed mesh, fast renderer, or passing software test does not establish a real binding, a conservation method, or fabrication readiness.
