## Limits

- Opening is an authored fixed-length spine-coordinate construction, not a calibrated paper/leather stress, moisture, friction or gravity solver. The neutral case curve retains its derived length; individual layers are not strain-solved.
- Five nested bifolia per gathering and visible sewing/link stitches are proposed. Every needle hole, adhesive microcontact and endband tie-down route is not certified. Endbands must not be claimed as load-tested structural bands.
- The placement checks cover paper/paper, outer paper/endpaper against boards, feet against slab and straps against paper. They do not establish universal whole-assembly collision freedom or continuous-in-time proof.
- Layer dimensions and workshop values are proposals from the governing brief. Leather, rag paper, patina and wear are procedural appearances. No museum facsimile, reflectance scan or manufactured dummy is claimed.
- Close-up typography remains an authored typeset manuscript interpretation. Six representative spreads carry content; other reading content is outside this study.
- V1 anatomy, old 55 mm-return measurements and old passing reports do not validate this rebuild. Their retained evidence is historical only.
