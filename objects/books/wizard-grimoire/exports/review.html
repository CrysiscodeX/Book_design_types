Construction review

What changed in the understanding

  • Size from the sewn, rounded and backed text block outward; do not force the block into a preselected cover envelope.
  • A leaf has two pages. A gathering may contain several nested folded sheets; do not equate every gathering with one loose sheet.
  • Keep paper, endpaper, board and applicable lining grain parallel to the spine; leather has directional response rather than a machine-paper grain.
  • Sewing swell belongs at the fold, not as a uniform air gap across every leaf.
  • Rounding creates a convex spine and concave fore-edge; backing creates shoulders matched to board thickness.
  • Boards rotate at their joint lines, outside the shoulder. A case spine is not an extra cover board or a long hanging loop.
  • Endbands follow the text-block spine. A stuck-on endband is decorative; a structural endband requires gathering tie-downs.
  • In case binding, mull flanges and endpaper hinges transfer the block load. Laced boards have an additional support-to-board load path.
  • Tight-back covering and spine flex together; hollow-back case and text-block spines are distinct surfaces separated by a tube/space.
  • A normal-offset closed leaf is required for inspection. Edge textures may replace subpixel rendering only when individual physical leaf identities remain.
  • Material response, moisture and gravity are not proved by an authored pose or a closed-mesh report.

Quality gate decisions

PASS — construction representation: separate assemblies, measured layer derivation, real joint lines, closed leaves, curved text spine, independent case neutral curve, hollow walls, supported covering and explicit board load path.

PASS — covered mechanical samples: actual-mesh paper tests, separate interface checks, measured curve-length checks and browser state controls. Coverage is recorded in machine-readable reports.

REWORK before manufacturing or claiming full physical realism: material calibration, a sewn physical dummy, complete hidden sewing/adhesive contact verification and constitutive paper/leather behaviour. Digital artwork and corner-fold finesse remain visual review topics.

Limits

  • Opening is an authored fixed-length spine-coordinate construction, not a calibrated paper/leather stress, moisture, friction or gravity solver. The neutral case curve retains its derived length; individual layers are not strain-solved.
  • Five nested bifolia per gathering and visible sewing/link stitches are proposed. Every needle hole, adhesive microcontact and endband tie-down route is not certified. Endbands must not be claimed as load-tested structural bands.
  • The placement checks cover paper/paper, outer paper/endpaper against boards, feet against slab and straps against paper. They do not establish universal whole-assembly collision freedom or continuous-in-time proof.
  • Layer dimensions and workshop values are proposals from the governing brief. Leather, rag paper, patina and wear are procedural appearances. No museum facsimile, reflectance scan or manufactured dummy is claimed.
  • Close-up typography remains an authored typeset manuscript interpretation. Six representative spreads carry content; other reading content is outside this study.
  • V1 anatomy, old 55 mm-return measurements and old passing reports do not validate this rebuild. Their retained evidence is historical only.
