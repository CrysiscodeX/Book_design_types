Physics and material behaviour

TopicInterpretation
GrainSpine-parallel paper, endpaper, board and applicable lining grain. Across-grain movement is greater; the source ratio 1.5–4 is approximate. Leather directional properties need their own treatment.
BendingTension outside a bent layer, compression inside, neutral axis between. D = E t³ / [12(1−ν²)] is a plate relation, not a calibrated anisotropic solver.
Throw-up and drapeThe spine changes shape and leaves bend toward the reading planes. The study authors these paths; it does not predict how a particular rag paper falls under gravity.
SwellThe proposed 3.3 mm sewing allowance belongs at the folds. Uniform leaf-to-leaf air must not be used as sewing swell.
MoistureBalance covering and pastedown drying loads. Keep grain compatible; board warping is a material/load issue, not an arbitrary visual noise effect.
GravityFor case bindings, the hinges and mull carry the hanging block. Large volumes require support. No calibrated fatigue, sag or humidity simulation is claimed.

Retired assumptions

  • independent board rotations about the spine centre
  • fixed 55 mm leather return as universal spine law
  • mixed case and laced-board load paths
  • uniform sewing swell at the fore-edge
  • one endband following the case spine
  • topology/FPS used as a craftsmanship score
