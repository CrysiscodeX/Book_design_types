# Water + Botanical Ornament + Sigilcraft — v0.2 Demonstration

**Status:** Proposed textual design demonstration, not a generated image, mesh, executable animation, tested workflow, or saved evaluation artifact.

## Shared identity

Primary Water; no accent elements; neutral fantasy motif; sigilcraft-and-warding style; a separate botanical ornament module. Botanical decoration does not automatically select Nature or Fae. Inspiration comes from book-01's geometry, book-04's delicate plant-like tracery, and book-08's layered framing; these new assignments are proposals.

Palette: deep blue `#173D54`, turquoise `#40C9D7`, pearl `#C6E9E7`, muted silver `#789CA6`, warm paper `#DCCDAF`. Smooth inlays contrast with fine matte cover grain. Water paths follow botanical veins while the ward boundaries remain readable. Use neutral fill with restrained cyan local accents.

A lower quiet label band holds title and flexible example labels: development stage "Implementation", project area "Data processing", industry "Research". These are illustrative labels and impose no rank or element restriction. Permanent rank markers and optional evidence-status inserts are separate.

## Envelope and ornament placement

Use face coordinates u across the cover from spine to fore-edge, and v from foot to head. Keep the central motif near the face center and the lower label band quiet. Frame, leaves, fixed fittings, and all relief remain inside L/H/B.

For this demonstration only, propose maximum combined fixed-relief allowances of 6 / 10 / 16 mm for Initial / Advanced / Meistro, leaving at most 44 / 70 / 104 mm for the covered body and internal mounting region. These are visual allocation limits, not selected board gauges, page-block thicknesses, or fabrication tolerances. Rear relief consumes the same combined allowance. Never place a full-depth body beneath an additional projecting medallion.

Reserve increasingly broad inner spacing as the frames grow; do not stretch one Initial ornament uniformly into the other ranks. The page block and cover-joint geometry remain separately designed in each future model.
