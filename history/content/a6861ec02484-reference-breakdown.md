## Cross-reference conclusions

- All eight have substantial page blocks; exact thicknesses and page counts are unknown.
- Books 01–03 emphasize geometric signs; 04–06 emphasize organic motifs; 07–08 emphasize solar assemblies.
- Fine cover lines should not be confused with the deep relief that changes silhouettes.
- Hardware is decorative-looking evidence until its actual attachment and operation are designed.
- Background props, lighting, camera blur, source markings, and airborne effects are separate from the book asset.
- Real-world sizes, unseen surfaces, working mechanisms, and motion require new specifications rather than image-derived claims.
