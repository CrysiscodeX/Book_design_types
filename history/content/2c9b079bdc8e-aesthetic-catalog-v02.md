# Curated Aesthetic Catalog — v0.2

Date: 2026-10-06. **Evidence: proposed.** This is an authored starter vocabulary, not an exhaustive canon of fantasy or an implemented model. Source observations remain in [the reference study](reference-breakdown.md).

## Layered choices and coverage

Twelve starter elements × three ranks = **36 elemental treatments**. Six fantasy motifs and eight wizardry styles each have a neutral option and modify these treatments. Combinations are not expanded into a fixed list of complete books. Development stage, project area, and industry remain flexible labels, independent of rank and appearance.

One primary and up to two distinct accents are allowed at every rank. Primary palette/motion leads; accents occupy subordinate ornament and the existing rank layer budget. Initial may express accents statically. Reserve quiet space for title, rank, content labels, and evidence status. Do not combine multiple competing central emblems.

Initial/Advanced/Meistro use one/three/five simultaneous elemental channels respectively. The layers below are motion appearances, not a requirement for one particle system per channel. Resting versions are restrained; active versions carry the rank's individual/routed/nested meaning. Shared outcome/status cues are evidence-gated and do not add persistent layers.

Defaults: Initial; Water; no accents; neutral motif/style; ambient resting. Water is a proposed starting choice, not an exclusive or required industry mapping.

## Authored combination recipes

| Recipe ID / name | Primary | Accents | Visual logic | Composition guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| steam — Steam | water | fire | Water dominates; warm fire accents create vapor-like overlays around flow crossings | Do not replace the flowing book surface with an opaque white cloud |
| molten-stone — Molten stone | fire | earth | Fire occupies mineral seams inside earth-like strata | Keep rigid strata and readable margins visible |
| sandstorm — Sandstorm | earth | air | Earth textures anchor directed air ribbons and sparse sand-like motes | Keep particle density low around labels |
| thunderstorm — Thunderstorm | air | lightning; water | Air controls circulation; lightning follows ordered routes; water appears in secondary droplets | No rapid full-scene flashes or label-obscuring rain |
| frost-bound-nature — Frost-bound nature | nature | ice | Living vine relief remains visible beneath selective frost tracery | Do not bury botanical identity under solid frost |

Across recipes, primary-led compositions roughly target 70/20/10 emphasis with two accents. This is an art-direction guide, not an exact color-mixing formula. With one accent, leave more quiet primary space rather than adding a missing third element. All recipes work at all ranks; recipe layers remain inside the 1/3/5 budget.

## Quality review matrix

For each of the twelve elements, review all three ranks in neutral, grayscale, effects-off, paused, and reduced-motion conditions. For each of the five recipes, review all three ranks for primary hierarchy and quiet label space. Review all six motifs and eight styles at least once with a non-obvious compatible element to expose accidental element coupling.

Maintain one focal center; distinct rank topology; silhouette detail only where it serves the design; controlled exposure; and separate content/evidence labels. Actual renders and physical samples are future checks, not verified by this document.
